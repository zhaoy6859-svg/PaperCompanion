# Companion illustrations

All whale-girl artwork was drawn by the app's owner and supplied on 2026-10-19
(a nine-pose chibi set, originally on a black background). The black background was
keyed out to transparency.

- `poses/pdf.png` — welcome hero, app icon source
- `poses/sparkle.png` — chat empty state
- `poses/stars.png` — reading-level sheet
- `poses/chart.png`, `poses/notebook.png`, `poses/marker.png` — welcome pose strip
- `poses/bar.png` — translation progress
- `poses/key.png` — model settings
- `poses/book.png` — notes
- `poses/pdf.png` face crop → `companion-avatar.png` (chat byline)

The app icon is generated from `poses/pdf.png` with `tools/make_icon_from_art.swift`.
